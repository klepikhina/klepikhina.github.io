Justin's parents have been talking about hiking Flattop Mountain from Bear Lake to Grand Lake for several years. So many years, that we decided to do it 20 years to the month from when they started talking about it. Little did they know that Justin had two bonus missions for them en route - Hallett Peak (bonus 1.2 mi) and Lake Nokoni (bonus 5 mi).

After a long previos day shuttling cars, we woke up bright and early to make sure we had plenty of time for ice cream after. We started from the Bear Lake trailhead around 4:45am.
