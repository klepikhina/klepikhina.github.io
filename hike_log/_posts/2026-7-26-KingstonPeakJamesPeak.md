Because we were up on this side of the CDT, we wanted to also do a quick hike up James Peak while we traversed the road. So, we got an early start - no time for oatmeal!

This part of the road was simultaneously less technical and more technical than the night drive. We had some more mellow traverses, and eventually, the crux.
